# 3D AutoShooter
Project Name: 3D Face Shooter
Team: Cesar Lozano Jr, Mark Munoz, Juan Del Angel

Project Overview: In 3D Face Shooter, players must survive a chaotic 3D environment for three minutes, proving all the haters wrong by shooting them in the face or dodging their attacks. The longer you survive, the more enemies appear, representing people trying to bring you down. If enemies get too close, they deal damage by hurling hurtful comments, lowering your health. Survive until the timer runs out to win and show that you can overcome negativity, but if you take too much damage, you “lose heart” and get sent back to the main menu, symbolizing the defeat caused by depression. The game challenges players to manage shooting, dodging, and staying alive in a tense and fast-paced 3D arena.

Mark Munoz – I added the main menu UI and implemented the player’s shooting mechanics, allowing bullets to hit enemies and deal damage.
